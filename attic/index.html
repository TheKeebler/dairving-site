Attic planner

Looking around

Drag empty space to turn the room. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag, Shift-drag, two fingers or the Slide button to slide the view. Arrow keys or WASD move. Double-click the floor to look there.

Furniture

Pick a piece from the bottom bar, or Custom size for something you found. Drag to move it. Click a piece to type its real width, depth and height in inches. R rotates, H hides, Delete removes. Red means it does not fit, and the bar says why.

Walk

W and S (or up and down arrows) walk. A and D (or left and right arrows) turn. Drag to look around. Double-click the floor to jump there. You cannot walk where the ceiling is below your eyes.

Stairs

Stair fit checks if a piece makes it up: type its size or pick it in the room and press Use selected piece. In Walk, you can walk down the stairs and back up.

Measure

Click two points. It snaps to the corners of pieces. Esc stops.

Undo

Ctrl or Cmd + Z.
